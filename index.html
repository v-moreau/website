About me

I am Vincent Moreau [vɛ̃sɑ̃ moʁo], a postdoctoral researcher in computer science working with Paweł Sobociński in the Compositional Systems and Methods Group at the Tallinn University of Technology, in close relation to the Logic and Semantics Group led by Tarmo Uustalu.

My office is ICT 225, and my email address is vincent.moreau@taltech.ee.

I am interested in the interface between mathematics and computer science. Here are a few topics I work on, and strive to connect together and understand from a categorical viewpoint:

  • Higher-order automata theory and model checking
  • The syntax and semantics of λ-calculi and type theories
  • Profinite spaces, Stone-type dualities, and codensity monads
  • Implicit automata, regular functions, and transducers
  • Linear logic, quantitative semantics, and the Taylor expansion of programs
  • Algebraic theories, universal algebra, and effects
  • Synthetic probability theory and Markov categories
  • Higher structures and homotopical algebra
  • Formal category theory and double categorical structures
  • Finite model theory and logical games

I will happily supervise internships — don't hesitate to email me if any of these topics interest you!

My Mastodon is @vimo, and my ORCID number is 0009-0005-0638-1363.

I got my engineering degree from Télécom Paris, and my PhD from Université Paris Cité.
